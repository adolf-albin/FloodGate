FLOODGATE CHALLENGES

Task 1 — DoS Fundamentals
Learn the fundamentals of Denial-of-Service attacks, service availability, legitimate traffic, and excessive requests.

Task 2 — Reconnaissance
Explore the FloodGate service and identify HTTP endpoints, ports, responses, and service information.

Task 3 — Traffic Analysis
Analyze controlled traffic and determine allowed requests, blocked requests, traffic percentages, and response patterns.

Task 4 — Detection
Use logs and source information to identify unusual traffic patterns from a defensive monitoring perspective.

Task 5 — Rate Limiting
Understand how rate limiting controls excessive requests and helps protect service availability.

Task 6 — Incident Investigation
Investigate the simulated event using traffic statistics and logs.

Task 7 — Final Challenge
Apply the skills learned throughout the room to complete a practical defensive investigation.
