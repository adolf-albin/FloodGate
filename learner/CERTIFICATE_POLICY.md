# FloodGate Certificate of Completion Policy

## Certificate Status

The FloodGate Certificate of Completion is locked by default.

A learner must complete:

- Task 1
- Task 2
- Task 3
- Task 4
- Task 5
- Task 6
- Task 7

All required questions must be completed.

Total requirement: **45 / 45 questions**.

## Unlock Rule

The certificate is automatically unlocked only after the server confirms that all seven tasks are complete.

The learner cannot unlock the certificate by changing the browser interface or directly requesting the download endpoint.

## Certificate

After successful completion:

- A unique certificate ID is generated.
- The certificate becomes available from the dashboard.
- The learner can download the PDF.
- The certificate can be verified using its certificate ID.

## Verification

Verification is available only for certificates that were actually issued by FloodGate.

## Scope

The FloodGate Certificate of Completion records completion of the FloodGate learning lab.

It is **not** an accredited professional certification, academic qualification, or government-issued credential.
