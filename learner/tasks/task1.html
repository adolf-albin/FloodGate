🛡️ Task 1 — Introduction: DoS Fundamentals

Learning Objectives

Before investigating an incident, a defender needs to understand the security concept behind it. A denial-of-service (DoS) condition occurs when excessive demand or resource consumption prevents legitimate users from accessing a service normally. The goal of this task is to understand the fundamentals before working with FloodGate's technical evidence.

🧪 Lab Steps

  1. Think about the three basic security goals: confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
  2. Consider what happens when a web service becomes too busy to serve legitimate users.
  3. Compare normal user traffic with unusually excessive traffic.
  4. Remember that unusual traffic is something to investigate; it is not automatically malicious.
