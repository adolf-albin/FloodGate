🔎 Task 2 — Reconnaissance

Learning Objectives

Reconnaissance is the process of discovering information about a service before deeper analysis. In this controlled lab you will identify the FloodGate service, inspect its HTTP response, and discover useful application endpoints.

🧪 Lab Steps

  1. Confirm that the training service is running.
  2. Use curl against the local service.
  3. Inspect the HTTP response and identify the server port.
  4. Visit the application's known endpoints and determine what each one provides.
  5. Do not modify the service or target anything outside the local laboratory.

🧪 Commands

curl -i http://127.0.0.1:5000/
curl http://127.0.0.1:5000/stats
curl http://127.0.0.1:5000/logs

⚠️ Controlled Lab

All testing must remain against the local FloodGate training service at 127.0.0.1. Do not modify the simulator to target external systems.
